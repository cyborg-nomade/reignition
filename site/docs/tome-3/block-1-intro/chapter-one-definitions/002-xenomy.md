# Xenomy

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/xenomy/){ .article-source }

Federico has kicked the living daylights out of me (on
[this](https://www.xenosystems.net/352/) thread), and only
the outer darkness remains. It’s a passage through singularity, so
mathematical consistency requires me to be
*infinitely* appreciative of that.

The idea of Neocameralism, drawing all its real functionality from
Exit, is parasitic upon what lies beyond it: the Patchwork of
competitive alternatives. Since an exterior disintegration does all
the work, why not fold the outside in?

It’s time to come out as a **Xenomist**. All power to
the Outside!
