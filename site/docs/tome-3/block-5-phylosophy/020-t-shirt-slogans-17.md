**Nothing lasts forever**

Stolen immediately from
[T-Zip](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2333804/), this kind of
crypto-nihilistic word game has an archaic classical
[pedigree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nothing_comes_from_nothing), is (weakly)
[anticipated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outis) in the
*Odyssey*, became an obsession
[among](https://www.enotes.com/shakespeare-quotes/nothing-can-come-nothing)
the Elizabethans, and contributed the engine of Heideggerian
fundamental
[ontology](https://eksistence.blogspot.com/2008/08/martin-heidegger-and-question-of-being.html). It still guides the *Outside in*
[reading](https://www.xenosystems.net/quote-note-112/) of
Milton, and no doubt much else besides. It hides a gnostic-skeptical
[metaphysics](https://www.uf-blog.net/epoche/) within a
commonplace resignation. Zero, time, and camouflage are bonded in
chaos. Make of it what you will …

[ADDED](https://www.zerohedge.com/news/2014-10-17/imf-and-austrian-theory): “The Austrian theory of the business cycle has never been a radical
premise. It only stipulates that any workaround of the natural cycle
of economic growth must come with ensuing costs. It’s a simple law:
you can’t get something for nothing. A majority of economists believe
the opposite. In other words, they believe in magic.”
