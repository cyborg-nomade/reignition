# Harsh, but true

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 4 - CYBERNETICS / SECTION A - INTELLIGENCE · February 28, 2013</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/harsh-but-true/){ .article-source }

[This](https://blog.jim.com/science/why-darwinism-is-more-controversial-than-ever.html/comment-page-1#comment-245388)
argument is both empirically and rationally impeccable:

*If you cooperate to kill and eat large animals, that is a lot
more cooperation than if you live on fruit, nuts, and insects.*

*If you cooperate to make war and genocide, that is a lot more
cooperation than if you cooperate to kill large animals.

Chimps and men kill and eat deer, monkeys and suchlike. Chimps and
men make war. Therefore the common ancestor of chimps and men made
killed and ate large animals, and made war – was a killer ape. The
ancestors of men are that branch of the lineage that ate meat more
heavily, the ancestors of chimps are that branch of the lineage
that ate meat less heavily.*

*Cooperative killing is the killer application for
intelligence.*
