# General Intelligence

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 4 - CYBERNETICS / SECTION A - INTELLIGENCE · March 11, 2016</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/general-intelligence/){ .article-source }

This still crops up occasionally as a ‘controversial concept’ so
it’s worth putting up a quick-and-easy docking-[port](https://quillette.com/2016/03/10/the-bermuda-triangle-of-science/)
to the *informed* mainstream position.

*… the evidentiary base regarding the existence of general
intelligence and its ability to predict important life outcomes —
including
[health](https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/psp/86/1/174),
[longevity](https://ije.oxfordjournals.org/content/early/2015/07/24/ije.dyv112.full)
and
[mortality](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/07481187.2015.1137994#.VuLQtPl96Uk), as well as other key
[variables](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0160289613000524)
— is beyond compelling, it’s
[overwhelming](https://www.nature.com/mp/journal/v20/n1/abs/mp2014105a.html). And if you find yourself feeling like you can do damage to this
evidence base by invoking arguments about “multiple intelligences”
or something of the sort, let me save you the effort. Those urges
illustrate unfamiliarity with any of the serious research done on
the topic in the last several decades. If those urges haunt you,
I’d recommend Stuart Ritchie’s
[excellent primer](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/1444791877/ref=as_li_ss_tl?ie=UTF8&linkCode=sl1&tag=quillette-20&linkId=50bed4c567d224a39ce191295d02c82c)
on the topic. The waters of intelligence research, though
controversial, no longer require that you be Magellan to navigate
them. As we will see below, however, it is only one small step
from banal psychometric work on IQ, to the mother-load of academic
controversy. Stay tuned. …*

For most here this will be redundant. The next (edgier) stage will
also be redundant. It’s posted here almost as much in appreciation
of its exasperated tone as for its linkage.

Gottfredson, cited in the post is the author of ‘Mainstream Science
on Intelligence’ (1994), still after more than two decades probably
the best short
[primer](https://www.udel.edu/educ/gottfredson/reprints/1997mainstream.pdf). The *Wikipedia*
summary is
[here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainstream_Science_on_Intelligence)
(with some commentary, and useful linkage).
