# A Correction

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 4 - CYBERNETICS / SECTION B - XENOECONOMICS / CHAPTER TWO - CAPITAL, THE THING · January 26, 2016</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/a-correction/){ .article-source }

Just noticed that I’ve been
[accused](https://forum.socialmatter.net/discussion/78/a-nick-land-primer-courtesy-of-grif-rutledge anthropomorphized capital)
of having “anthropomorphized capital” (by NBS). Gnon, no!

The point is this: If you think there’s a difference between
capitalism and artificial intelligence you’re not seeing either at
all clearly. The Austrians already understood that capitalism is
an information processing system, and the decentralized robotics /
networks types on the other side grasp that AI isn’t going to
happen in a research lab. ‘Anthropomorphism’ has nothing to do
with it. Complex Adaptive Systems are the place to start.

If you even vaguely understand what a convergent wave is, you’ve
got most of what you need to discuss the topic, but if you haven’t
read
[this](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/butler-samuel/1872/erewhon/ch23.htm)
classic you’re probably wasting everyone’s time.

[ADDED](https://onlineopen.org/capital-thinks-too): A

most Neoreactionary musings in the dust).
