# Uncanny Valley

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 4 - CYBERNETICS / SECTION B - XENOECONOMICS / CHAPTER SEVEN - AI · July 8, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/uncanny-valley/){ .article-source }

State-of-the-art in Japanese
[android](https://vimeo.com/59110465) design. (Thanks to
[@existoon](https://twitter.com/existoon) for the
pointer.)

It’s not really — or even remotely — an AI demonstration, but it’s
a demonstration of something (probably several things).

[![uncanny_2](https://i2.wp.com/www.xenosystems.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/uncanny_2.jpg?resize=778%2C534)](https://i2.wp.com/www.xenosystems.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/uncanny_2.jpg)

Wikipedia
[provides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncanny_valley)
some ‘Uncanny Valley’ background and links. The creepiness of
*The Polar Express* ([2004](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0338348/)) seems to have been the trigger for the concept going
mainstream.

From the level of human body simulation achieved already, it’s
looking as if the climb out to the far side of the valley is close
to complete. Sure, this android behaves like an idiot, but we’re
used to idiots.

[ADDED](https://www.technologyreview.com/news/528796/neuroscientists-object-to-europes-human-brain-project/): Some hints on how the inside out approach is going (and
[speculations](https://mitrailleuse.net/2014/07/01/conscious-machines/)).
