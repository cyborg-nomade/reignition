Given the price flatline over the half-century to 1973, it’s not
easy to be confident that the market has settled into a steady
rhythm, but the investment side of the oil business certainly
seems to have:

[![Oil00](https://i0.wp.com/www.xenosystems.net/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Oil00-e1422348892992.jpg?resize=666%2C422)](https://i0.wp.com/www.xenosystems.net/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Oil00-e1422348892992.jpg)

([Via](https://www.zerohedge.com/news/2015-01-26/why-oil-prices-will-be-lower-longer-3-simple-charts).)

Something like two decades of low energy prices ahead, if the
established pattern is prolonged. There’s either a valuable
futurist building-block there, or a provocation for futurological
discussion.
