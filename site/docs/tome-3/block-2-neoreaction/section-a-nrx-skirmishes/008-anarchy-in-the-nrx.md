# Anarchy in the NRx

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION A - NRX SKIRMISHES · February 18, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/anarchy-in-the-nrx/){ .article-source }

Arthur R. Harrison (@AvengingRedHand) makes the incisive
observation: “Well the thing is NRx is a specific kind of
post-libertarianism, or it was. Now it seems to be just a name for
reaction post-Moldbug.” There could be people who don’t see that as
degeneration. In fact, it seems there are.

Reactotwitter is lurching into sheer delirium (as \*ahem\*
[forecast](https://www.xenosystems.net/2014-a-prophecy/)).
To begin with, it seems no longer to concur on what it begins with:

> Evola outranks Moldbug. The accomplishments and credentials of the
> former are far higher.
>
> — Michael Anissimov (@MikeAnissimov)
> [February 17, 2014](https://twitter.com/MikeAnissimov/statuses/435553631140454400)

(Not in my army.)

It’s time to choose your own tradition and slap an NRx sticker on
it. Is anyone envisaging any limits to this:

> [@Outsideness](https://twitter.com/Outsideness)
> [@MikeAnissimov](https://twitter.com/MikeAnissimov)
> Whose ideas have more specifically influenced NRx? At this point,
> it's whoever influences its writers.
>
> — Bryce Laliberte (@AnarchoPapist)
> [February 18, 2014](https://twitter.com/AnarchoPapist/statuses/435595841793826816)

> [@AnarchoPapist](https://twitter.com/AnarchoPapist)
> [@MikeAnissimov](https://twitter.com/MikeAnissimov)
> Since there are no entry controls, this is a formula for complete
> intellectual anarchy. (OK, Mad Max it is.)
>
> — Outsideness (@Outsideness)
> [February 18, 2014](https://twitter.com/Outsideness/statuses/435635329094012931)

> [@Outsideness](https://twitter.com/Outsideness) Moldbug
> isn't here. Neoreaction is a free for all of our own making.
>
> — Michael Anissimov (@MikeAnissimov)
> [February 18, 2014](https://twitter.com/MikeAnissimov/statuses/435595537237032960)

So NRx is a formless anarchy telling the world how to put itself in
order? Actually, I think this is probably right, and theoretically
interesting, but it clearly needs thinking about. How can there
imaginably be an ‘entryism’ threat when command control is a teeming
chaos? What does this *example* of radical disorder suggest?

Here’s the NRx anarcho-chaos already pouring through the pipe:

> [@MikeAnissimov](https://twitter.com/MikeAnissimov)
> [@AnarchoPapist](https://twitter.com/AnarchoPapist)
> [@Outsideness](https://twitter.com/Outsideness) I
> respect Moldbug, but he is one of many. We all have our voice – we
> can and should add our ideas
>
> — Anti Democracy Blog (@antidemblog)
> [February 18, 2014](https://twitter.com/antidemblog/statuses/435597446366785536)

Everyone has a voice, and we respect that … oh wait …
