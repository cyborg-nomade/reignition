# White Out

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION A - NRX SKIRMISHES · December 13, 2013</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/white-out/){ .article-source }

[According](https://www.counter-currents.com/2013/12/the-dark-enlightenment-is-new-right-lite/#more-44046)
to the White Nationalist fraternity, the Dark Enlightenment tends to
like civilized people even when they aren’t *really white*. I
think that’s right (and Right), although — of course — it’s supposed
to be a problem.

It’s certainly amusing that the only people who don’t think we’re
Nazis are the Nazis. They recognize that “cognitive elitists” are
inherently prone to race treachery — which could be pushed all the
way out to species treachery (if I have anything to do with it).
*Optimize for intelligence* isn’t any kind of key to racial
solidarity, or solidarity of any other kind. Even HBD, they
generally insist, isn’t *them* (it’s too attentive to PISA
[ratings](https://www.oecd.org/pisa/keyfindings/pisa-2012-results-overview.pdf)
and such). There are some seriously interesting controversies
implicit in all this, although rage is likely to break them up
before they get very far. It makes me realize that one thing I
appreciate about the Neoreaction is its anger management, which is
inextricable from its taste for irony (and probably also from its
decadence).

At *Amos & Gromar*
[there’s](https://amosandgromar.wordpress.com/2013/12/11/the-new-right-versus-neoreaction-no-evola-quotes-arent-good-enough/)
some worthwhile comment, and commentators.

Boundaries should always be appreciated, whoever is drawing them.
