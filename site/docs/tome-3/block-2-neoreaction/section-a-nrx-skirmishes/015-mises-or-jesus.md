# Mises or Jesus?

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION A - NRX SKIRMISHES · July 2, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/mises-or-jesus/){ .article-source }

There’s been a lot of
[this](https://www.frontporchrepublic.com/2009/10/the-one-salvation-of-ludwig-von-mises/)
kind of thing around recently. It’s mainly been arriving in a link
storm from Wagner Clemente
[Soto](https://twitter.com/wcsoto), who’s too
unambiguously Throne-and-Altar in orientation to identify as NRx or
333, so it’s probably an exercise in internal discipline taking
place in another camp. Still, it’s difficult not to ask: Could this
be the next fission pile building up?

[Here](https://mises.org/books/lastknight.pdf)‘s a link
to Jörg Guido Hülsman’s (excellent)
*Mises: The Last Knight of Liberalism*, which seems to have
provided the background citations for the recent round of attacks.
(This agitation always takes me back to
*[Der](https://modernism.research.yale.edu/wiki/index.php/The_Magic_Mountain)
[Zauberberg](https://www.greatbooksguide.com/the_magic_mountain)*.)
