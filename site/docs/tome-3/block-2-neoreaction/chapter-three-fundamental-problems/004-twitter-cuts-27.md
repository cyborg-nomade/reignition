*This* (cubed).

It shouldn’t even be difficult. Could any ‘rectification of names’
be more straightforward? If the word is grasped with any lucidity,
*the more diversity the better*. Every problem that the
(non-totalitarian) right has with ‘diversity’ is in fact a rejection
of homogenization. To allow the prevailing pattern of usage to
continue unchallenged is an absurdity.

‘Diversity’ already tilts into non-universality, and that is
meta-level rightism itself.

The diversity between diversity and non-diversity is the best
diversity.
