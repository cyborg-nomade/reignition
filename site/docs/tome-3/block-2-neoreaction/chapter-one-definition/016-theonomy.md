# Theonomy

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / CHAPTER ONE - DEFINITION · August 14, 2015</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/theonomy/){ .article-source }

[This](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Reconstructionism)
is the NRx sect that still hasn’t shown up. (The
[slot](https://www.xenosystems.net/trichotomy/) is wide
open.) A critical but informative
[essay](https://www.firstthings.com/article/1990/05/002-why-wait-for-the-kingdom-the-theonomist-temptation)
at *First Things* explains:

*Bible law requires a radical decentralization of government under
the rule of the righteous. Private property rights, especially for
the sake of the family, must be rigorously protected, with very
limited interference by the state and the institutional church.
Restitution, including voluntary slavery, should be an important
element of the criminal justice system. A strong national defense
should be maintained until the whole world is “reconstructed”
(which may be a very long time). Capital punishment will be
employed for almost all the capital crimes listed in the Old
Testament, including adultery, homosexual acts, apostasy,
incorrigibility of children (meaning late teenagers), and
blasphemy, along with murder and kidnapping. There will be a cash,
gold-based economy with limited or no debt. These are among the
specifics broadly shared by people who associate themselves with
the theonomic viewpoint.*

(‘Triggered’ by [this](https://esr.ibiblio.org/?p=5238) —
which is well worth re-visiting.)

ADDED: This is worth spelling out (from the same essay) —

*A reconstructed world ruled by future Rushdoonyites will not,
needless to say, be democratic. Rushdoony is straightforward in
condemning democracy as a “heresy.” He writes that he is in
agreement with John Dewey on the proposition that “supernatural
Christianity and democracy are inevitably enemies.”*
