# Ace Torches the Popcorn Stand

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION E - CRITIQUE OF CONSERVATISM · March 19, 2015</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/ace-torches-the-popcorn-stand/){ .article-source }

Forget the headline, which is just a pretext — this
[post](https://ace.mu.nu/archives/355602.php) is really
something. A couple of highlights:

*They think that our desire for a better America will draw us to
vote for the Least Worst candidates. […] But many of us now feel
like the Communists, or the hardcore paleocons: There really is
not a large enough difference between the two parties any more to
**bother oneself in terms of emotional and financial investment
any longer**. […] Either way, we will have some form of repressive,
unresponsive socialism in this country; what should we care
whether the National Welfare Depot is painted in Red or Blue? […]
I’m not coming back. I’m done. For the past ten years of my life,
I’ve made arguments, some of which I knew to be false, to defend
and apologize for the GOP; I see now that I was a fool to do
so.*

And:

*Here’s Some Truth: We all know this, but being Part of the Team,
I felt obligated to lie, because I figured you expected me to lie,
even though you didn’t believe it. […] So yeah: The GOP is never
repealing Obamacare or even trying hard to do so. They will make
false efforts at doing so which they can present to voters as a
Good College Try, but aw shucks, we couldn’t quite do it. […] It’s
a relief to no longer have to propagate this obvious, feeble lie.
[…] I know very few of you believe the GOP has much intention of
repealing Obamacare, but, being a Republican, I have previously
felt the need to present The Official Party Position even knowing
it was total bullshit. […] I’ve known it was a lie for a year,
which is why I hate when it comes up on, say, the Podcast. What am
I supposed to say? Am I supposed to pretend the GOP is going to
repeal it? […] So they’re not. They never were. […] It was always
a lie.*

If there was ever a moment to stoop to a ‘wow, just wow!’ this might
be it.
