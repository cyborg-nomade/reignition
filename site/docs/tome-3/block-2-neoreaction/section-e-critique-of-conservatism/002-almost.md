# Almost …

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION E - CRITIQUE OF CONSERVATISM · September 26, 2013</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/almost/){ .article-source }

As a symptom of things hitting the buffers,
[this](https://pjmedia.com/michaelwalsh/2013/09/25/the-turning-point/?singlepage=true)
Michael Walsh article is vaguely encouraging. It speaks unreservedly
about the “collaborationist Republican Party” but eventually loses
itself in the pseudo-conundrum:

*How a political party cannot sell Freedom and Liberty and Leave
Me Alone to a formerly free people is beyond me …*

Could it perhaps be because democratic party politics has
exhaustively demonstrated its incompatibility with “Freedom and
Liberty”, “Leave Me Alone”, and a “free people”?
