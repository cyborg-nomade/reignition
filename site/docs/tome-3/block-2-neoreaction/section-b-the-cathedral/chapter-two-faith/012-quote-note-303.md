# Quote note (#303)

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION B - THE CATHEDRAL / CHAPTER TWO - FAITH · November 14, 2016</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/quote-note-303/){ .article-source }

Trump is unintelligible, in an interesting
[way](https://quillette.com/2016/11/12/donald-trump-and-the-failure-of-mainstream-social-science/):

[President Elect Donald J. Trump is]
*tapping into a broad resistance to contemporary moral beliefs,
beliefs that have become increasingly institutionalised over the
past fifty years. […] The problem is that these are precisely
the beliefs that are held above inquiry in the social
sciences.*

Not just a political crisis, then, but an epistmeological crisis
(precisely *because* it will be so difficult for the
dominant social organs of knowledge to accept the fact).
