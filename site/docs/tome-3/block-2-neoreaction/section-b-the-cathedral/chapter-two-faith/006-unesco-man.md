# UNESCO Man

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/unesco-man/){ .article-source }

Via [Cussans](https://codeless88.wordpress.com/) (dark
channels), comes
[this](https://vbn.aau.dk/en/publications/unesco-man(e189ab10-c052-11dd-a5a8-000ea68e967b).html)
crucial document on the intersection of racial anthropology and
international institutional politics. The abstract:

*From 1945 and the following 20 years UNESCO – the United
Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization – was
at the heart of a dispute in international scientific circles
over the correct definition of the concept of race. This was
essentially a dispute about whether the natural sciences or the
social sciences should take precedence in determining the
origins of human difference, of social division and of the
attribution of value. The article provides an overview of the
work on race carried out by UNESCO, examines the measures it
took to combat racism, pays special attention to their political
and social impact in various member states, and demonstrates how
UNESCO played a major part in imposing a new view of man: UNESCO
Man.*
