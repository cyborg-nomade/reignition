# Tribal Epistemology

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION B - THE CATHEDRAL / CHAPTER TWO - FAITH · July 29, 2015</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/tribal-epistemology/){ .article-source }

When you know who people identify with, you generally get a
full-spectrum insight into their beliefs for free.

As Fernandez
[puts](https://pjmedia.com/richardfernandez/2015/07/23/dependence-day/#more-44171)
it:

*… while Western civilization pays lip service to “evidence
based” policy, in practice most human beings rely on
[social proof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_proof)
to decide what to believe. … The search for “social proof” as a
determinant of conviction is not wholly crazy. Few of us can say
why a pharmaceutical works. But if the doctor prescribes a pill,
we drink it without question. Most of the world is preoccupied
with making a living and consequently have a high level of
[rational ignorance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_ignorance). “Rational ignorance occurs when the cost of educating oneself
on an issue exceeds the potential benefit that the knowledge
would provide.” It takes too long for us to figure things out
from first principles, so we find a “smart man” and do what he
tells us.*

While everybody is compelled to economize in this way to some
extent, skepticism — in its many different varieties — offers a
measure of practical defense. (One variant is simply the
heuristic, inherited by all Protestant clades, if quite commonly
left idle by them, of
*looking things up for yourself*.)

“What do you do if the Church has been hijacked by demons?”
[asked](https://thefutureprimaeval.net/why-methodists-dont-go-to-heaven/)
Harold Lee. This is exactly the same concern, raised from another
angle, and escalated towards its essence. As trust in the
machineries of critical truth production is
[eroded](https://theden.tv/2014/05/13/rejecting-science-for-equality-nicholas-wade-and-feminist-biology/), in direct
[proportion](https://wattsupwiththat.com/2015/01/20/2014-the-most-dishonest-year-on-record/)
to their
[Cathedralization](https://foseti.wordpress.com/2013/06/05/the-cathedral-goes-viral/), the primary tendency is to tribalize ‘knowledge’ (as a signal
of belonging), and secondarily to promote a general nihilism, on
the ever-more plausible assumption that everything we have ever
been told is a lie.

This is how a civilization is burnt to the ground. By selling
their souls to the New Church, all epistemologically-relevant
social institutions trade authority for mere
[power](https://devinhelton.com/post-1507220057/historical-amnesia), or the capacity to command tribal allegiance and conformity. In
response, *trustlessness* is
[installed](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf) as the
foundational principle of realistic socio-political analysis, or
informally manifested in a spreading and deepening cynicism. What
little exists of counter-knowledge is mostly sheer refusal, or
opportunistic deference to the enemy’s enemy. No
[Antiversity](https://unqualified-reservations.blogspot.hk/2009/10/gentle-introduction-to-unqualified.html)
exists. It too is invoked, in the interim, only as a refusal. Its
entire meaning, up to this point, is that
*we don’t any longer believe what we’ve been told*.

We remember enough about what Science once was, or what
market-honed economic signals were, to know that tribal
epistemology is cognitive garbage. As we slide down the slope,
increasingly, it’s the garbage heap in which we all live.
