# View From the Left

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION B - THE CATHEDRAL / CHAPTER SEVEN - THE DECLINE BEGINS · July 9, 2013</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/view-from-the-left/){ .article-source }

Claus Offe lucidly
[explains](https://www.eurozine.com/articles/2013-02-06-offe-en.html)
what the proponents of ‘solidarity’ are
~~hoping for~~ utterly hopeless about in Europe. The entire
article is so thoroughly saturated in doom-drenched, soul-scouring
melancholia that by the end I was searching for Odysseus-style
restraints to prevent myself doing a wild happy-dance around the
office. From the Euro-progressive perspective, things look
*seriously* bleak.

As a bonus, there’s a great gloss on
[degenerative ratchets](https://www.xenosystems.net/the-idea-of-neoreaction/): “… those fatal errors which, once committed, prove
irreversible, closing off any return to the
*status quo ante*.” By carrying everything relentlessly to
the brink, they’re more of a nightmare for the perceptive left
than they are for us. By this stage in history, the left has much
more to lose. It’s their regime that is going over the cliff.
(Yes, I realize this reboot-friendly *Schadenfreude* will
earn a spanking from Goulding.)

[ADDED](https://www.spectator.co.uk/features/8950741/watch-out-france-nicolas-sarkozy-is-back-and-he-wants-revenge/):
*France is in its worst shape for more than three decades, since
François Mitterrand nearly blew up the economy in the early
1980s trying to stimulate growth through government deficits and
nationalisations. Unemployment is at 10.5 per cent and climbing.
The economy is contracting. And overseeing the shambles is the
suety, confidence-draining face of François Hollande*.
