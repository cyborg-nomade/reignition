The
[supply](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2015/feb/17/author-uk-dream-job)
side could be reasonably compared to a high-pressure fire-hose:

*A new
[poll for YouGov](https://yougov.co.uk/news/2015/02/15/bookish-britain-academic-jobs-are-most-desired/)
of almost 15,000 people found that 60% would like to be an
author. The news may come as a surprise to the bestselling and
critically acclaimed novelist Sebastian Faulks, who this weekend
[expressed his wish to find a job](https://www.theguardian.com/books/shortcuts/2015/feb/15/six-alternative-jobs-sebastian-faulks), writing in the Spectator that he has “now spent almost a
quarter of a century alone in a garret staring at a blank wall,
and I think it has driven me a bit mad”. […] … According to a
survey
[carried out by Digital Book World](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2015/jan/23/authors-earnings-fall-350pa-survey)
earlier this year, almost a third of published authors make less
than $500 (£350) a year from their writing.*
