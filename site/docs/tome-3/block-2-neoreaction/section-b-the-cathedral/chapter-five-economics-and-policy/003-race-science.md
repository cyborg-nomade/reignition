[essay](https://radishmag.wordpress.com/2014/02/02/crazy-talk/)

experimentation on human guinea pigs (aka ‘pajama ferrets’):

**[cure for racism](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007%2Fs00213-012-2657-5/fulltext.html)**.*
