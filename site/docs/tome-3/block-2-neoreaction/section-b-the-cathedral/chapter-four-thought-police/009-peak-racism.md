# Peak Racism?

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION B - THE CATHEDRAL / CHAPTER FOUR - THOUGHT POLICE · August 11, 2013</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/peak-racism/){ .article-source }

The
[witch](https://radishmag.wordpress.com/2013/08/09/volume-3-issue-5-black-magic/)–[craze](https://blog.jim.com/culture/radish-explains-what-racism-means.html)
seems to be running out of juice, according to
[some](https://alternativeright.com/blog/2013/8/2/the-racism-bubble)
thought-provoking Ngram data organized by Brad Trun.

*The charge of “Racist!” is losing its sting as its overzealous
hurlers increasingly render it farcical. “Racist” is, for the
first time since the neologism’s inception 80 years ago,
starting to fall out of favor. Zooming in on the post–1930
period in Google Ngram Viewer and eliminating smoothing reveals
that “racist” references topped out as the calendar switched to
the new millennium.*

My welcome news receptors are so corroded, that I can’t help
wondering: what’s wrong with this story?

(In other news, Peak African is still some way
[off](https://www.washingtonpost.com/blogs/worldviews/wp/2013/07/16/the-amazing-surprising-africa-driven-demographic-future-of-the-earth-in-9-charts/). Caplan will no doubt be thrilled. Does anybody sensible think
that a billion Nigerians by 2100 sounds like a future that might
work? It’s probably a racist question, but you have to do what you
can for dying traditions.)
