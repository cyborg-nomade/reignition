# Shrunken Brains

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION B - THE CATHEDRAL / CHAPTER FOUR - THOUGHT POLICE · April 1, 2015</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/shrunken-brains/){ .article-source }

Gregory Cochran brusquely
[dispatches](https://westhunt.wordpress.com/2015/03/31/scanners-live-in-vain/)
what might be the most incompetent piece of ‘scientific’ reasoning
in recent years (although the competition for that honor gets ever
more intense). The discovery — brains of poor children are
statistically smaller. The insane leftist inference passed into
the public realm as a logical conclusion:
*poverty shrinks brains*. I’m not going to insult XS
readers by laboring over the mistake here (Cochran does it
succinctly enough, and with appropriate biting contempt). It’s
utterly horrifying, from any remotely objective viewpoint, that
such blatant stupidity could ever borrow the robes of science,
even momentarily. This is what collapse looks like (and most
probably our brains are shrinking).

(I was aiming to do some kind of April Fool’s thing here today.
Sadly, this isn’t one.)

[ADDED](https://drjamesthompson.blogspot.hk/2015/03/income-brain-race-and-big-gap.html): Thompson patiently picks through the mess. “The paper and the
comments will lead readers to believe that lack of money is
stunting the brains of poorer children. This is possible, but not
proved by this study because of obvious genetic confounders.”
