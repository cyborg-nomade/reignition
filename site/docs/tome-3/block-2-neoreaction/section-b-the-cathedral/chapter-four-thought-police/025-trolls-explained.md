# Trolls Explained

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION B - THE CATHEDRAL / CHAPTER FOUR - THOUGHT POLICE · August 15, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/trolls-explained/){ .article-source }

If, like this blog, you have been benighted enough to understand
Internet trolls as abusive
[irritants](https://www.xenosystems.net/trolls/),
masters of
[disguise](https://www.xenosystems.net/lord-of-the-trolls/),
[satirists](https://www.xenosystems.net/chaos-patch-19/), or even amusing
[pets](https://www.xenosystems.net/pet-trolls-1/), you
apparently need a good talking to. Farhad Manjoo writing in
(surprise!) *The New York Times* has a lesson you need to
hear. Trolling, it turns out, has a very simple explanation — it
is exactly identical to a Political Incorrectness. To be a troll
is in fact simply not being a progressive.

Citing Doctor Whitney Phillips, of Humboldt State University, and
a troll expert (who has written a
[book](https://billions-and-billions.com/2014/02/13/my-book-officially-forthcoming-with-mit-press/)
on the subject), Manjoo illuminates the phenomenon unambiguously:

*If there’s one thing the history of the Internet has taught us,
it’s that trolls will be difficult to contain because they
really reflect base human society in all its ugliness.
[Trolls find a way](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SkWeMvrNiOM).*

*“It’s not a question of whether or not we’re winning the war on
trolling, but whether we’re winning the war on misogyny, or
racism, and ableism and all this other stuff,” Dr. Phillips
said. “Trolling is just a symptom of those bigger problems.”*

As with so very many other things, there’s no solution to trolling
short of the absolute triumph of progressive across the whole of
the earth. This is an argument crying out for an
[#AAA](https://www.xenosystems.net/aaa/) tag like no
other I’ve ever seen. (I’d link the Twitter
[hashtag](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23AAA&src=typd), but it’s deeply confusing.)
