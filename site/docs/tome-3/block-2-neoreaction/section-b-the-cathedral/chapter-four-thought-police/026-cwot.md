# CWoT

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION B - THE CATHEDRAL / CHAPTER FOUR - THOUGHT POLICE · August 21, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/cwot/){ .article-source }

The Cathedralist War on Trolling is limbering up fast. Just a few
days ago, we had
[this](https://www.xenosystems.net/trolls-explained/).
(Paraphrased: to resist the Cathedral is trolling).
[Now](https://www.pcmag.com/article2/0,2817,2463211,00.asp)
the follow up (“Trolls are like terrorist cells” — literally).

The [Duck](https://twitter.com/jokeocracy) does the
integration:

> 1) disagreeing with progressivism is trolling 2) trolling is
> terrorism therefore 3) disagreeing with progressivism is
> terrorism
>
> — Duck Enlightenment (@jokeocracy)
> [August 20, 2014](https://twitter.com/jokeocracy/statuses/502127848912850945)

That escalated quickly.

*They’re everywhere and even if one gets eliminated, there’s two
more to take its place (that also applies to HYDRA). But I feel
like this is the point we’re at now. That’s sad and terrible,
but it’s the truth. I used to think turning comments off was
\*the\* solution, and while I do think comments have become
useless, and largely a hotbed for hate and racism, turning them
off is only going to drive the poison to even more public forums
like Twitter and Facebook, where a hateful or factually corrupt
tweet or status update can spread like a disease across the
globe and turn supposed rational human beings into muckrakers of
misinformation, hate, and other dark things.*
