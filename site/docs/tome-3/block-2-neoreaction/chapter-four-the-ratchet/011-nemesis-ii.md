Less than a year after
[surrendering](https://blog.mozilla.org/blog/2014/04/03/brendan-eich-steps-down-as-mozilla-ceo/)
corporate governance to SJWs,
[this](https://www.computerworld.com/article/2893514/an-incredibly-shrinking-firefox-faces-endangered-species-status.html)

causality here. Nevertheless, as a dramatic exemplification of harsh
Cosmic Law it’s going to be difficult to beat.
