Briggs [captures](https://wmbriggs.com/post/15913/) the
essentials:

*You have to love — I do — how the cessation of accelerating
profligate spending is called in Europe “austerity”. Here [in
the UK] the
slow-down-in-speeding-up-yet-still-increases-in-spending are
called “budget cuts”.*

The “let’s carry on decaying at a genteel pace chaps” party
[won](https://www.bbc.com/news/election/2015/results)
(unexpectedly). Insurgent parties did badly (except at the
*geographical* — rather than ideological — fringe). A
*status quo* outcome, then. A shallower, longer decline
path it is …

The more positive implications concern territorial
[disintegration](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/general-election-2015/politics-blog/11590878/British-politics-is-now-about-two-existential-issues-Scotland-and-Europe.html). Deepening political alienation in Scotland, and a commitment to
a referendum on Europe, promise opportunities for multi-level
secessionary tides to strengthen.

Also, the left will go even more nuts. When the teeth-gnashing
commentary begins to roll in, I’ll try to link some as
*Schadenfreude* tonic.
