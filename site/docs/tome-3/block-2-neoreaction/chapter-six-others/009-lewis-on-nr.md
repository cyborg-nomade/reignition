# Lewis on NR

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / CHAPTER SIX - OTHERS · January 6, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/lewis-on-nr/){ .article-source }

Matt K. Lewis, in *The Week*, shows that a critical
[appraisal](https://theweek.com/article/index/254690/why-are-us-conservatives-so-obsessed-with-monarchies?utm)
of Neoreaction really doesn’t require hysteria. (The second half of
the article is especially impressive.) If the custodians of
Cathedral orthodoxy don’t find a way to punish him for his sobriety,
this piece could set a new standard for public discussion of the
anti-democratic right.

*… these movements tacitly accept that conservatism as a political
force is utterly incapable of slowing the leftward march of
liberalism. By definition, conservatives, who want to conserve the
good things about the past, are always playing defense. When you
consider that many of my conservative views aren’t terribly
different from John F. Kennedy’s views in 1960, this becomes
self-evident.*

Can this degree of honesty be allowable?
