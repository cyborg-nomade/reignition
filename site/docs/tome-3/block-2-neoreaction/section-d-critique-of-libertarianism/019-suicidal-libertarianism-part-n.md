# Suicidal Libertarianism (Part-n)

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 2 - NEOREACTION / SECTION D - CRITIQUE OF LIBERTARIANISM · December 2, 2013</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/suicidal-libertarianism-part-n/){ .article-source }

[Two](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2013/12/arnold-klings-bad-demographic-news-for-libertarians.html)
[posts](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2013/12/u-s-immigrants-attitudes-toward-libertarian-values.html)
in succession at Tyler Cowen’s
*Marginal Revolution* acknowledge that libertarianism’s
suicide-by-population-replacement is proceeding according to
spontaneous disorder. Completely un-shockingly, mass low-IQ
immigration from dysfunctional cultures that despise economic
liberty has pushed libertarian ideas from marginality into complete
irrelevance. So it goes.

Firstly,
[there](https://www.arnoldkling.com/blog/bad-demographic-news-for-libertarians/)‘s “Bad Demographic News for Libertarians” from Arnold Kling. It
should probably be noted that this isn’t a story being told from an
immigration-catastrophe angle, so anybody with advanced skills at
mental segmentation can dismiss it as irrelevant. You need to check
the final table of the source
[post](https://conversableeconomist.blogspot.com/2013/11/changes-in-americas-family-structure.html), by Timothy Taylor, to connect the dots. Kling’s sober conclusion:
“I am afraid that the number of households married to the state has
soared.”

Secondly, Cowen
[cites](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2234200)
this paper by Hal Pashler (a psychologist at UCSD), whose research
“results showed a marked pattern of lower support for pro-liberty
views among immigrants as compared to US-born residents. These
differences were generally statistically significant and sizable,
with a few scattered exceptions. With increasing proportions of the
US population being foreign-born, low support for libertarian values
by foreign-born residents means that the political prospects of
libertarian values in the US are likely to diminish over time.”

I just wish there had been some sort of short-cut to self-abolition
for
[these](https://econlog.econlib.org/authorbcaplan.html#recent)
[maniacs](https://openborders.info/) that hadn’t been
routed through the destruction of America.
