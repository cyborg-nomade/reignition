*If it sounds strange to say that libertarianism is “white,” well,
it’s still true. Libertarianism is, empirically, really
[goddamn white](https://www.thedailybeast.com/articles/2013/10/30/libertarians-the-great-white-hope.html), and some have suggested that that
[may not be a coincidence](https://www.salon.com/2015/06/10/why_libertarianism_is_so_popular_on_the_right_its_the_last_bastion_of_white_male_dominance/): That is, libertarianism makes assumptions about what’s normal
for everyone on the basis of the white experience. Normally,
that’s a point made by the left as a criticism, but the whiteness
of libertarianism is increasingly accepted by post-libertarian
reactionaries like Moldbug as a badge of honor. It could also
indicate a wider trend in the future, if a combination of
demographic changes and political projects to “[make whiteness visible](https://www.nafsa.org/uploadedFiles/Chez_NAFSA/Connect_and_Network/Community/NAFSA_Regions/Region_XI/Conference/InterculturalMakeWhitenessVisible_Stowe2013_Smtih.pdf)” lead more white people to think of cultural values like
individual rights as tied to whiteness, rather than as universal
principles. Certainly Trump’s brand of nationalism seems to rest
on doing something similar with the idea of “America,” abandoning
any pretense to a
[creedal](https://nplusonemag.com/issue-24/politics/race-and-the-american-creed/)
idea of national identity in favor of one based on race. These
trends could well produce, among whites, more conscious
anti-racists
**and** conscious racists at the same time.*
