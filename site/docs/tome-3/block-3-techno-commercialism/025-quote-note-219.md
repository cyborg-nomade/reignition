# Quote note (#219)

<p class="article-meta">Tome III · Xenosystems / BLOCK 3 - TECHNO-COMMERCIALISM · February 9, 2016</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/quote-note-219/){ .article-source }

[This](https://www.azquotes.com/quote/747203) notorious
Andrew Mellon quote — disastrously
[ignored](https://econlog.econlib.org/archives/2013/04/krugman_gets_ho.html)
by Herbert Hoover — might be the XS most favored recommendation of all
time (in the realm of political economy, at least):

*Liquidate labor, liquidate stocks, liquidate farmers, liquidate
real estate. It will purge the rottenness out of the system. High
costs of living and high living will come down. People will work
harder, live a more moral life. Values will be adjusted, and
enterprising people will pick up from less competent people.*

Anyone who has conniptions about it (which is almost everyone) is part
of the problem. Mellon still understands entropy dissipation. No one
in a position of political authority has since.

NRx (*Outside in* version) is the obstreperous alternative
history in which Mellon was listened to.
