# Ebola-Chan

<p class="article-meta">Tome IV · Abstract Horror / BLOCK 5 - HYPERSTITION · September 20, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/ebola-chan/){ .article-source }

[![140731Ebola-jpg](https://i1.wp.com/www.xenosystems.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/140731Ebola-jpg.jpg?resize=699%2C783)](https://i1.wp.com/www.xenosystems.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/140731Ebola-jpg.jpg)

[This](https://www.vocativ.com/world/nigeria-world/ebola-4chan-anime/)
was my gateway into the horror-tracts of Ebola-Chan. It was
immediately obvious that something of great significance was
happening.  
Upsetting (for those still nursing shreds of humane moral intuition):
certainly, and deliberately.  
Meaningful: beyond question, and massively.

The ebola
[trendline](https://america.aljazeera.com/articles/2014/9/8/who-thousands-newebolaliberia.html)
is currently
[exponential](https://www.zerohedge.com/news/2014-09-09/ebola-outbreak-doubles-3-weeks-who-warns-conventional-means-control-not-working). Richard Fernandez places the phenomenon in its proper intellectual
[context](https://pjmedia.com/richardfernandez/2014/09/12/the-prince-and-the-vizier/). Whatever else the outbreak may be — a human (and economic)
[catastrophe](https://america.aljazeera.com/watch/shows/inside-story/articles/2014/9/17/can-wealthier-nationshelpafricastoptheebolavirus.html)
for West Africa, a
[threat](https://www.kurzweilai.net/1-in-5-chance-ebola-will-spread-to-the-us-in-september)
to the
[West](https://www.zerohedge.com/news/2014-09-15/20-chance-ebola-usa-october-277124-global-cases-year-end-model-predicts)
— it is also a revelation (or ‘apocalypse’ in the strict sense). It’s
a Khan Academy
[demonstration](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra2/exponential_and_logarithmic_func/exp_growth_decay/v/exponential-growth-functions)
for slow and reluctant learners. Such things lend themselves to
spontaneous religious interpretation.

It wasn’t supposed to look like this:

[![500px-Ebola-chan_meme](https://i0.wp.com/www.xenosystems.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/500px-Ebola-chan_meme.jpg?resize=500%2C387)](https://i0.wp.com/www.xenosystems.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/500px-Ebola-chan_meme.jpg)

The (rough) coincidence with the death of 4chan is — in itself — a
topic of abysmal fascination. I’m kicking that can for the moment.
There’s much on
[this](https://www.xenosystems.net/4chan-furor/) precursor
discussion thread of relevance.

For now, some preliminary indications as to why this might be thought
to matter (immensely), in revisable order of gravity:

(1) Readers of John Michael Greer are prepared for socio-economic
decline to be accompanied by an eruption of religious
[abnormality](https://www.xenosystems.net/deep-ruin/). For
anybody with a taste for irony (a crucial epistemological disposition,
in the opinion of this blog), there is much nourishment to be found in
the Ebola-Chan phenomenon. Most prominently, despite — and more
probably *because of* — the
[shocking](https://www.tumblr.com/search/ebola%20chan)
[racism](https://www.587board.com/bbs/show_topic/34012-4chan-racists-are-stoking-ebola-fear-in-nigeria-with-this-anime-meme)
propelling it, Ebola-Chan opens a cross-cultural
[plane](https://www.nairaland.com/1906849/white-demon-worshippers-trying-spread#26369908)
of
[communication](https://www.ibtimes.co.in/ebola-cia-created-demon-conspiracy-theory-goes-viral-meme-shows-disease-created-by-white-people-609490)
completely absent from the ‘responsible’ Western responses to the
plague. It makes sense of ebola in a way that is far closer to the
sensibility of its target populations than the lofty medico-globalist
pronouncements of legitimated authorities. Ebola-Chan packages the
outbreak for a folk-religious
[response](https://beforeitsnews.com/paranormal/2014/09/ebolachan-cult-of-ebola-workers-of-nergal-sickness-and-pestilence-shall-come-2475688.html)
— of exactly the kind it is being met with in the villages it (or
‘she’?) ravages.

(2) Ebola-Chan is a demoness of communication, not only across
cultures (West Africa, America, Japan …) but also between cultural and
biological patterns of virulence. The ‘meme’ — as a more-or-less exact
analog of the gene — is unleashed on 4chan in *sympathy* with
the virus. It connects with the biological contagion, in various ways,
through relations of copying (transcription), prolongation, and
promotion. Ebola-Chan is a crossing.

(3) As memotechnics, Ebola-Chan condenses an accumulated stock of
practical heuristics. Its genre is, most immediately, that of the
chain-letter — conveying a message pre-adapted to spreading.
*Copy me, or be punished* (stricken). It’s darkly humorous,
cruel, ironical, and self-reflective, but at the level of memotechnics
none of this undermines anything. If “I love you Ebola-Chan” spreads
as a joke — it still spreads. The Nigerian email
[scam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/419_scams)
[industry](https://www.motherjones.com/politics/2014/03/what-i-learned-from-nigerian-scammers)
attests to the inter-cultural consistency of this memotechnic plane.

(4) Ebola-Chan is already operating as a factor in Fourth-Generation
Warfare. It complicates the pacification
[efforts](https://voxday.blogspot.com/2014/09/the-ebola-exponent.html)
of the ‘international community’ in unpredictable ways. Once again,
certain peculiar cultural
[formations](https://therapybook.wordpress.com/2014/08/02/ebola-turning-out-to-be-joint-bio-and-psych-warfare-assault-from-george-soros-and-bill-melinda-gates/)
are waiting to connect with it. At this stage it is difficult to reach
even the preliminary stage of a lucid analysis, but clearly the
memotechnic militarization of a medical emergency is an obstacle to
the smooth evolution of established management procedures. The WHO is
not ideologically equipped to publicize its intervention within the
context of an occult religious
[race](https://www.thenewsnerd.com/technology/cdc-says-ebola-vaccine-works-white-people/)
war.

(5) To what extent is Ebola-Chan an avatar of globalized
[Helter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helter_Skelter_(Manson_scenario))
[Skelter](https://www.crimelibrary.com/serial_killers/notorious/manson/skelter_6.html)? Surely not to no extent at all?

(6) As 4chan is pushed ever deeper into the shadows, it seems
reasonable to assume that its practical alliance with memotechnic
chaos will also deepen. In this respect, Ebola-Chan is the Yin to the
Cathedral’s Yang — a complement, fed by subterranean conservation
laws. Much prophetic density accompanies such an analysis. If it is an
end, it is no less the beginning of an end.
