# Quote note (#213)

<p class="article-meta">Tome IV · Abstract Horror / BLOCK 2 - ABSTRACT HORROR / SECTION A - HORRORISM / CHAPTER TWO - PRACTICE · January 30, 2016</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/quote-note-213/){ .article-source }

Bolivarian Socialism has made a truly crucial
[contribution](https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/wonk/wp/2016/01/29/venezuela-is-on-the-brink-of-a-complete-collapse/)
to Marxist-Leninist-Guevara-Penn-Chávez Thought — the idea of
*food-line rationing*:

*Venezuela’s government has tried to deny economic reality with
price and currency controls. The idea was that it could stop
inflation without having to stop printing money by telling
businesses what they were allowed to charge, and then giving
them dollars on cheap enough terms that they could actually
afford to sell at those prices. The problem with that idea is
that it’s not profitable for unsubsidized companies to stock
their shelves, and not profitable enough for subsidized ones to
do so either when they can just sell their dollars in the black
market instead of using them to import things. That’s left
Venezuela’s supermarkets without
[enough food](https://www.npr.org/sections/parallels/2015/10/29/452636462/the-nightmare-of-grocery-shopping-in-venezuela), its breweries without enough hops to
[make beer](https://news.vice.com/article/venezuela-faces-looming-beer-shortage-in-dispute-with-nations-biggest-brewer), and its factories without enough pulp to
[produce toilet paper](https://edition.cnn.com/2013/09/21/world/americas/venezuela-toilet-paper/). The only thing Venezuela is well-supplied with are lines.*

*Although the government has even started rationing those,
[kicking people out of line](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2015-01-12/venezuela-lines-shorten-as-police-reduce-shoppers-not-shortages)
based on the last digit of their national ID card.*

The genius of that. You think anyone should just be allowed to
stand in a food-line, bourgeois imperialism-style? The Revolution
has moved beyond such reactionary ideas.

(The Gnon-bliss XS receives from this regime is hard to
communicate without wandering into tentacle-porn.)
