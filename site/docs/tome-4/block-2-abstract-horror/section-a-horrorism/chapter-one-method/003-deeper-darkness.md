# Deeper Darkness

<p class="article-meta">Tome IV · Abstract Horror / BLOCK 2 - ABSTRACT HORROR / SECTION A - HORRORISM / CHAPTER ONE - METHOD · November 18, 2013</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/deeper-darkness/){ .article-source }

At the point where people have begun to
[talk](https://www.voxeu.org/article/accounting-great-divergence)
about “a positive Black Death effect” do they realize how far
they’ve descended into the shadows? The hard-core horror of
Malthusian analysis always has some new depths to fathom.

The idea that European living standards rose following the
‘relief’ from Malthusian pressure gifted by bubonic plague is far
from new. It is even something approaching an uncontroversial fact
of economic history. To take an additional step, however, and
attribute the rise of the West to its mid-14th century epidemic
devastation, is to wander into unexplored tracts of icy
[misanthropy](https://econlog.econlib.org/archives/2012/07/social_darwinis.html). *Europe was lucky enough to have enough people die*.

The Malthusian implication ([systematized](https://www.econ.ucdavis.edu/faculty/gclark/a_farewell_to_alms.html)
by Gregory Clark) that only downward social mobility is compatible
with eugenic trends, is a dark thought I have touched upon
[occasionally](https://www.xenosystems.net/the-monkey-trap/), but have yet to firmly fix upon. The idea of mass population
destruction as a developmental gift, in any situation where
economic growth rates fall below average fertility (I simplify),
takes Dark Enlightenment to a whole other level.

As a footnote, it raises the question: was the Great Divergence
eugenic for the Far East (which fell behind) and dysgenic for the
West (which forged ahead)? Is economic prosperity essentially a
gene trasher?

I tend to side with libertarians in their aversion to (Keynesian)
[broken window](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broken_window_fallacy)
economics, but it is to be expected that such reasoning will
promptly subside into sheer cognitive paralysis when the far more
disturbing Malthusian conclusions are introduced. Libertarians
already think they’ve ‘got’ Malthus, as the guy who lost the
Simon-Ehrlich
[wager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon%E2%80%93Ehrlich_wager)
— an anti-capitalist green prophet preaching population
restriction.

The real Malthus is going to come as a shock. He certainly
spine-chills me.
