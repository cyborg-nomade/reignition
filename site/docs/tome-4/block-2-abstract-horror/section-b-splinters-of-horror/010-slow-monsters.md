# Slow Monsters

<p class="article-meta">Tome IV · Abstract Horror / BLOCK 2 - ABSTRACT HORROR / SECTION B - SPLINTERS OF HORROR · February 27, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/slow-monsters/){ .article-source }

One major lesson from Cambodia (previously noted) is that trees do
tentacle horror better than cephalopods — though in slow motion. I
think these snaps from Ta Prohm, Ta Som, and Preah Khan make the
point quite slitheringly. (They can all be enlarged by clicking.)
