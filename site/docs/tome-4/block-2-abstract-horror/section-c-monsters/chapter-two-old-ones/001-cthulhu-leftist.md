# Cthulhu, leftist?

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/cthulhu-leftist/){ .article-source }

[Really](https://unqualified-reservations.blogspot.com/2009/01/gentle-introduction-to-unqualified.html)?

Caught in the slipstream of tentacled abomination, as we are, the
question is an involving one. Is the spiral into a “[holocaust of freedom and ecstasy](https://www.dagonbytes.com/thelibrary/lovecraft/thecallofcthulhu.htm)” a leftist maelstrom? That seems plausible, even unavoidable, if
the right defines itself in opposition to
[chaotic evil](https://unqualified-reservations.blogspot.com/2007/05/what-if-theres-no-such-thing-as-chaotic.html). But if poly-tendrilled monstrosities from the Outside aren’t
our natural allies, what the hell are we doing among these
squares? It’s simply fate and allegiance from where we’re
slithering: If it’s a squid-shaped horror out of deep time, with
an IQ in four digits or more, and unspeakable plans for mankind,
then it’s one of ours, and — more to the point — we’re its.
