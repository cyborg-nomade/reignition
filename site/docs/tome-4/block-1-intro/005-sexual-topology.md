# Sexual Topology

<p class="article-meta">Tome IV · Abstract Horror / BLOCK 1 - INTRO · April 1, 2017</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/sexual-topology/){ .article-source }

Galen’s theory that the sexual organs were related by analogy,
converted into a mnemonic for medical students (as reproduced in
Siddhartha Mukherjee’s
[The Gene](https://www.amazon.com/Gene-Intimate-History-Siddhartha-Mukherjee/dp/1476733503)). It will confirm everyone’s worst suspicions, but that can’t be
helped.

*Though they of different sexes be  
Yet on the whole, they’re the same as we  
For those that have the strictest searchers been  
Find women are just men turned outside in.*

Mukherjee adds the question:
*… what force was responsible for turning men “inside out,” or women
“outside in,” like socks?*
