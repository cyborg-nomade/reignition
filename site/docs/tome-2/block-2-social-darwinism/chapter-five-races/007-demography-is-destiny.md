# Demography is Destiny

<p class="article-meta">Tome II · The Dark Enlightenment / BLOCK 2 - SOCIAL DARWINISM / CHAPTER FIVE - RACES · April 16, 2015</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/demography-is-destiny/){ .article-source }

For a blast of sudden, icy clarity,
[this](https://www.vdare.com/articles/john-derbyshire-ray-wolters-the-long-crusade-and-vdare-com?stf=two%20million%20copies)
is worth recalling:

*After decades of American Ed theorists and politicians grumbling
about our low ranking on international tests, we now know that, as
[Steve Sailer summarized in 2010](https://www.vdare.com/articles/pisa-scores-show-demography-is-destiny-in-education-too-but-washington-doesnt-want-you-to-k), reviewing the PISA (Program for International Student
Assessment) results from the previous year:  
\* Asian Americans outscored every Asian country, and lost out only
to the city of Shanghai, China’s financial capital.  
\* White Americans students outperformed the national average in
every one of the 37 historically white countries tested, except
Finland (which is, perhaps not
[coincidentally](https://www.vdare.com/posts/peasant-uprising-in-finland), an
[immigration restrictionist](https://www.vdare.com/posts/finnish-content-there-are-now-enough-immigrants-to-finland-that-we-can-tell-their-education-sy)
nation where whites make up about 99 percent of the
population).  
\* Hispanic Americans beat all eight Latin American countries.  
\* African Americans would likely have outscored any sub-Saharan
country, if any had bothered to compete. The closest thing to a
black country out of PISA’s 65 participants is the fairly
prosperous oil-refining Caribbean country of Trinidad and Tobago,
which is roughly evenly divided between blacks and South Asians.
African Americans outscored Trinidadians by 25 points.*

Racially disaggregate a conundrum that has tortured progressive
education reformers for over a century, and it entirely disappears.
Non-discrimination is mental and cultural chaos.
