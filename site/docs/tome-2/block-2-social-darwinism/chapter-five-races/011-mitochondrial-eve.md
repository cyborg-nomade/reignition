# Mitochondrial Eve

<p class="article-meta">Tome II · The Dark Enlightenment / BLOCK 2 - SOCIAL DARWINISM / CHAPTER FIVE - RACES · October 9, 2013</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/mitochondrial-eve/){ .article-source }

Without wanting to set off the usual suspects,
[this](https://www.nytimes.com/2013/10/09/science/ashkenazi-origins-may-be-with-european-women-study-finds.html?hp&_r=0)
[research](https://www.nature.com/ncomms/2013/131008/ncomms3543/full/ncomms3543.html)
into Ashkenazi ancestry is fascinating. Based on MtDNA analysis, it
is evident that: “Overall, at least 80 percent of Ashkenazi maternal
ancestry comes from women indigenous to Europe, and 8 percent from
the Near East, with the rest uncertain, the researchers estimate.”

Jewish matrilineal cultural descent starts to look extraordinarily
[odd](https://westhunt.wordpress.com/2013/10/08/jewish-moms/). Also, a final goodbye to Koestler’s Khazar hypothesis.
