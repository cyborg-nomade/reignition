# Fish People

<p class="article-meta">Tome II · The Dark Enlightenment / BLOCK 2 - SOCIAL DARWINISM / SECTION A - CLADISTICS / CHAPTER TWO - ETHNO-CULTURAL CLADES · July 6, 2016</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/fish-people/){ .article-source }

Since the opportunities for XS to agree (in
[advance](https://www.xenosystems.net/cladistic-meditations/)) with PZ Myers don’t come along too regularly, it’s worth
seizing upon
[those](https://freethoughtblogs.com/pharyngula/2016/07/02/yes-you-are-a-fish/)
that do. For anyone who thinks cladistics are important, this
point is worth strongly defending:

*There are multiple meanings of “fish”. We can use it to refer
to specific species or an extant category of animals: salmon are
fish, halibut are fish, herring are fish. No one objects to
that, and they all understand that if I said “humans are still
salmon”, that would be wrong. […] But another way the term is
used is as a descriptor for a clade. A taxonomic clade is a
[“grouping that includes a common ancestor and all the
descendants (living and extinct) of that ancestor”](https://evolution.berkeley.edu/evolibrary/search/imagedetail.php?id=260). […] So, for instance, humans belong to the mammalian clade,
which includes mice and cats and cows. If we have transhuman,
part-cyborg descendants, they will still be mammals, because,
note, by definition a clade must include all the descendants of
an ancestor. We’re trapped! There’s no way our progeny can exit
the clade!*

In fact, it’s such a sound point, it’s worth
[generalizing](https://www.xenosystems.net/ethno-cladistics/).
