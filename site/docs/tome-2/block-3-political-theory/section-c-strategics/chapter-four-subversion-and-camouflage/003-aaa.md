# AAA …

<p class="article-meta">Tome II · The Dark Enlightenment / BLOCK 3 - POLITICAL THEORY / SECTION C - STRATEGICS / CHAPTER FOUR - SUBVERSION AND CAMOUFLAGE · July 22, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/aaa/){ .article-source }

… stands for *agree, amplify, and accelerate*. Initiated
[here](https://countnothingface.wordpress.com/2014/07/20/the-three-as-an-anti-political-political-strategy/), and escalated
[here](https://www.newinternationaloutlook.com/2014/07/21/expansion-on-aaa/), it opens an unexplored horizon for strategic discussion within
NRx. No analysis of cultural conflict on the Internet can bypass a
reference to
[trolling](https://www.xenosystems.net/chaos-patch-19/),
and no understanding of trolling is any longer complete without
reference to AAA. It raises the discussion of
[parody](https://www.xenosystems.net/poes-law/) to a new
level. (If it isn’t already obvious, this blog is seriously
impressed.)

AAA works if strategic complication has favorable consequences.
Whichever cultural faction has the greater capacity for the
tolerance of difficulty, identity confusion, irony, and humor,
will tend to find advantage in it. I think that’s us. It’s
inherently toxic to zealotry.

As a sub-theme — but one keenly appreciated here — it marks a
critical evolution in the Cthulhu Wars. (Check out the graphics on
the TNIO post for recognition of that.) Rather than arguing over
whether “Cthulhu
[swims](https://unqualified-reservations.blogspot.hk/2009/01/gentle-introduction-to-unqualified.html)
[left](https://blog.jim.com/politics/cthulhu-swims-only-left.html)” AAA proposes amphetaminizing the monster regardless.
*If a “holocaust of freedom” is what you want, let’s go
[there](https://www.dagonbytes.com/thelibrary/lovecraft/thecallofcthulhu.htm)*. Take this operation to the end of the river … and see what we
find.
