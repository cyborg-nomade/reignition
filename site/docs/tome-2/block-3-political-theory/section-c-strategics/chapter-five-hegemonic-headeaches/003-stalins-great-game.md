# Stalin’s Great Game

<p class="article-meta">Tome II · The Dark Enlightenment / BLOCK 3 - POLITICAL THEORY / SECTION C - STRATEGICS / CHAPTER FIVE - HEGEMONIC HEADEACHES · October 2, 2013</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/stalins-great-game/){ .article-source }

Either Stalin played the Anglosphere like a  cheap piano in World
War Two, or something altogether more sinister was going on.
Foseti
[clarifies](https://foseti.wordpress.com/2013/10/01/review-of-the-last-lion-by-paul-reid/)
the conundrum beautifully:

*When the US finally joins the war, it does so with – as best as
one can decipher – only a few clear war aims: 1) demanding
unconditional surrender (of Germany and Japan – aka the only
bulwarks against Soviet domination of post-war Europe and Asia);
2) establishing the United Nations; and 3) ending European
(excluding Soviet) colonialism.*

*If you, gentle reader, can come up with a list of war aims that
would be more destructive to mankind at the time than those, the
next round is on me. Perhaps entirely coincidentally (or perhaps
not) these aims would seem to all work towards the direct
benefit of the Soviets. It’s almost like Soviets were making US
foreign policy.*
