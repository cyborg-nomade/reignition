[Harsanyi](https://thefederalist.com/2016/11/22/dear-media-please-dont-normalize-alt-right/):

***… institutional media and white nationalists have formed a
politically convenient symbiotic relationship***.

Is this seriously deniable?

There’s a lot of conspiracy-theorizing underway right now, but it
seems implausible, and superfluous. A spontaneous convergence of
(perceived) interests is capable of explaining everything. In the
end, though, someone is being played. In fact, it’s not only
possible, but probable, that both sides of this particular
arrangement are being played, and not primarily by each other.
