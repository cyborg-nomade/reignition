# The Islamic Vortex (Note-5)

<p class="article-meta">Tome II · The Dark Enlightenment / BLOCK 3 - POLITICAL THEORY / SECTION C - STRATEGICS / SEQUENCE ii - THE ISLAMIC VORTEX · June 2, 2015</p>

Michael Klare takes a
[look](https://www.tomdispatch.com/blog/176003/) at the
Islamic State logistics train (bullish for US defense stocks):

*In the years after invading Iraq and disbanding Saddam
Hussein’s military, the U.S. sunk about
[$25 billion](https://www.nytimes.com/2014/06/13/world/middleeast/american-intelligence-officials-said-iraqi-military-had-been-in-decline.html?_r=0)
into “standing up” a new Iraqi army. By June 2014, however, that
army, filled with at least
[50,000 “ghost soldiers,”](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/middle_east/investigation-finds-50000-ghost-soldiers-in-iraqi-army-prime-minister-says/2014/11/30/d8864d6c-78ab-11e4-9721-80b3d95a28a9_story.html)
was only standing in the imaginations of its generals and
perhaps Washington. When
[relatively small numbers](https://www.independent.co.uk/voices/iraq-crisis-capture-of-mosul-ushers-in-the-birth-of-a-sunni-caliphate-9530600.html)
of Islamic State (IS) militants swept into northern Iraq, it
collapsed, abandoning
[four cities](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2014/jun/11/mosul-isis-gunmen-middle-east-states)
— including Mosul, the country’s second largest — and
[leaving behind](https://www.dallasnews.com/news/local-news/20150519-fleeing-iraqi-troops-abandon-u.s.-tanks-artillery-humvees-to-islamic-state.ece)
enormous stores of U.S. weaponry, ranging from tanks and Humvees
to artillery and rifles. In essence, the U.S. was now standing
up its future enemy in a style to which it was unaccustomed and,
unlike the imploded Iraqi military, the forces of the Islamic
State proved quite capable of using that weaponry without a
foreign trainer or adviser in sight.*

*In response, the Obama administration
[dispatched](https://www.nytimes.com/2014/11/08/world/middleeast/us-to-send-1500-more-troops-to-iraq.html)
thousands of new advisers and trainers and began shipping in
[piles of new weaponry](https://www.tomdispatch.com/post/175943/tomgram%3A_peter_van_buren,_the_military-industrial_complex_in_iraq/)
to re-equip the Iraqi army. It also filled Iraqi skies with U.S.
planes armed with their own munitions to
[destroy](https://blogs.reuters.com/great-debate/2014/08/18/how-much-it-costs-the-u-s-to-blow-up-captured-u-s-military-hardware-in-iraq/), among other things, some of that captured U.S. weaponry. Then
it set to work standing up a smaller version of the Iraqi army.
Now, skip nearly a year ahead and on a somewhat lesser scale the
whole process has just happened again. Less than two weeks ago,
Islamic State militants took Ramadi, the capital of Anbar
Province. Iraqi army units, including the elite American-trained
[Golden Division](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/middle_east/chaos-in-iraqi-forces-contributed-to-islamic-states-biggest-win-this-year/2015/05/22/cf4e000e-ffd4-11e4-8c77-bf274685e1df_story.html), broke and fled, leaving behind — you’ll undoubtedly be
shocked to hear — yet
[another huge cache](https://www.military.com/daily-news/2015/05/20/isis-captures-hundreds-of-us-vehicles-and-tanks-in-ramadi-from-i.html)
of weaponry and equipment, including tanks, more than 100
Humvees and other vehicles, artillery, and so on.

The Obama administration reacted in a thoroughly novel way: it
immediately began shipping in new stocks of weaponry, starting
with
[1,000 antitank weapons](https://www.nytimes.com/2015/05/21/world/middleeast/us-to-send-rockets-to-iraq-for-isis-fight.html), so that the reconstituted Iraqi military could take out
future “massive suicide vehicle bombs” (some of which,
assumedly, will be those captured vehicles from Ramadi).
Meanwhile, American planes began roaming the skies over that
city, trying to destroy some of the equipment IS militants had
captured.*

*Notice anything repetitive in all this — other than another a
bonanza for U.S. weapons makers? Logically, it would prove less
expensive for the Obama administration to simply arm the Islamic
State directly before sending in the air strikes.*
