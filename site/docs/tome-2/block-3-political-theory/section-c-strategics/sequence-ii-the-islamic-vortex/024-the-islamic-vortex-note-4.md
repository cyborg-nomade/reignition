So the Islamic State has
[executed](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-middle-east-31121160)
their captive Jordanian pilot, Lt Moaz al-Kasasbehby, by
*burning him alive*. The event was artfully videotaped and
maximally publicized. It was an act undertaken with an
extraordinary degree of intent.

The ‘organization’
[beheaded](https://www.newyorker.com/news/daily-comment/isis-murdered-kenji-goto)
Japanese journalist Kenji Goto a few days previously. It had
[already](https://www.jpost.com/Middle-East/ISIS-executes-second-Japanese-hostage-389585)
beheaded another Japanese hostage, Haruna Yukawa, a week before.

The deliberate combination of indiscriminate and exorbitant
violence is remarkable. It looks like a purposeful escalation
beyond terror, aimed calmly at the entire world.
