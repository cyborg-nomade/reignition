Something like: “It is a fierce beast. When it is attacked, it
bites.” No doubt one of my cultivated readers can help.)

This elusive aphorism is driving me slowly insane. The
[closest](https://www.wordola.com/wusage/wife-to-be/p13.html)
I can get right now: “A French philosopher once said that a dog is
the most dangerous animal in the world because when it is attacked
it bites.” (Voltaire?)
