# Cnut the Great

<p class="article-meta">Tome II · The Dark Enlightenment / BLOCK 3 - POLITICAL THEORY / SECTION A - REPUBLICANISM AND CONSTITUTIONS / CHAPTER ONE - SOVEREIGNTY · March 3, 2016</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/cnut-the-great/){ .article-source }

According to legend, at least,
[Cnut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cnut_the_Great)
was the wisest of all kings, precisely because he ironized the
attribution of sovereignty.

“Surely, Great King, you are ominipotent
[Fnargl](https://unqualified-reservations.blogspot.hk/2007/05/magic-of-symmetric-sovereignty.html)
himself!”  
“Let us then
[test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Canute_and_the_waves)
the claim, shall we?”

Modern macroeconomics is the systematized refusal to learn from
this story. Sovereignty does not rise above the waves.
