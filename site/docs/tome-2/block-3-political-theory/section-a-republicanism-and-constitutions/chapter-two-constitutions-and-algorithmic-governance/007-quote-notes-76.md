<p class="article-meta">Tome II · The Dark Enlightenment / BLOCK 3 - POLITICAL THEORY / SECTION A - REPUBLICANISM AND CONSTITUTIONS / CHAPTER TWO - CONSTITUTIONS AND ALGORITHMIC GOVERNANCE · April 27, 2014</p>

Not a new point in this neck of the woods, but
[formulated](https://www.counter-currents.com/2014/04/fear-of-a-white-rancher/)
with exceptional elegance:

*There are only two possibilities regarding the Constitution of
the United States. One is that it is working as it was intended,
in which case it is a monstrosity. The other is that it was
broken somewhere along the way – in which case it failed.*

The prod back to this topic is appreciated, because it really
hasn’t been properly processed yet. (This blog has yet to do more
than stick a
[tag](https://www.xenosystems.net/the-odysseus-problem/)
on the problem.) Insofar as constitutions are at least partly
functional, they are *involved in the production of power*.
As abstract engineering diagrams for regimes they should no more
be expected to rule than rocket blueprints are expected to blast
into space — but they matter.
