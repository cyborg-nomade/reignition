This is an argument I’m really not grasping:

Libertarians are unrealistic because the world was once vastly
freer than it is today, and then progressively rolled down the
populist hill into the present social democratic latrine trench,
[so](https://unqualified-reservations.blogspot.com/2007/12/why-i-am-not-libertarian.html)
“Why would we expect different results on the second go?” [OK,
still following so far] … *thus* we need Kings back,
because … [we need to catch the rising tide, after all, the world
hasn’t ever been more monarchist than now? Prussian Neocameralism
outlasted Manchester Liberalism? Royalist institutions have
demonstrated their inherent immunity to the forces of
[decay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles,_Prince_of_Wales)? …]

How can reactionaries criticize free republics for falling apart?
Everything reactionaries have ever respected fell apart.
*Nobody would be a reactionary if their favored configuration of
the world hadn’t fallen apart*.

Republics are extremely fragile. All the more reason to take
devoted care of them (first of all, by protecting them from
democracy).
