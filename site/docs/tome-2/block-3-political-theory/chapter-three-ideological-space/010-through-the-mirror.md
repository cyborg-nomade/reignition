# Through the Mirror

<p class="article-meta">Tome II · The Dark Enlightenment / BLOCK 3 - POLITICAL THEORY / CHAPTER THREE - IDEOLOGICAL SPACE · April 25, 2015</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/through-the-mirror/){ .article-source }

The articulate Left
[comes](https://www.newleftproject.org/index.php/site/article_comments/strange_freedom_the_origins_of_the_neoliberal_leviathan)
close to capturing NRx from the other side, mapping out a persuasive
genealogy (through games theory and public choice analysis). The
final line of this piece gets closest:

*It is the logical endgame of a dark political vision crafted in
opposition to democratic advances; the realisation of a strange
freedom which lies at the root of the neoliberal dystopia, from
which the political establishment offers no deliverance.*

… except, they think counter-democratic darkness is already in
power, in the guise of ‘neoliberalism’, and that the populist
political charade, with its 40%+ state absorption of economic
product, financial central planning, and publicly-promoted
egalitarian evangelism, is an outcome compatible with the triumph of
a disillusioned right. It seems an absurd sticking point to reach —
that in the end, we can’t even agree about who is ruling the world.
