# The Eurasian Question

<p class="article-meta">Tome II · The Dark Enlightenment / BLOCK 3 - POLITICAL THEORY / SECTION B - FASCISM / CHAPTER THREE - EURASIANISM, THE ALT-RIGHT AND CURRENT EVENTS · August 7, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.uf-blog.net/the-eurasian-question/){ .article-source }

Within the great spans of history, domestic ideological
controversy is something close to a luxury good. Whenever it isn’t
to same extent ‘on hold’ the global environment is untypically
benign. Under more normal — which is to say *stressed* —
conditions, it either folds down into pragmatism, or explodes into
cosmic, eschatological drama. In today’s unmistakably stressed
world, Alexander
[Dugin](https://www.uf-blog.net/war-without-end/)‘s ‘[Eurasianism](https://www.4pt.su/en/topics/eurasianism)‘ exemplifies the latter eventuality.

[![Dugin01](https://www.uf-blog.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/Dugin01.jpg)](https://www.uf-blog.net/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/Dugin01.jpg)

As with Jacobinism and Bolshevism before it, Eurasianism matters
to you whether you want it to or not. The grandeur of its scope is
undeniable. It is concerned with nothing less than the fate of the
the earth. In this sense, nothing that anyone cares about falls
outside it. (People are beginning to get
[scared](https://www.nationalreview.com/article/372353/eurasianist-threat-robert-zubrin).)

Shelving moral and partisan responses, it is merely realistic to
acknowledge that Dugin is an ideological genius of the first
order. Synthesizing Russia’s native Eurasianist
[traditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurasianism)
with geopolitcal
[theory](https://www.mindef.gov.sg/safti/pointer/back/journals/1998/Vol24_3/8.htm)
and [deep](https://www.sacred-texts.com/atl/) currents
of occult
[mythology](https://atlanteangardens.blogspot.hk/2014/04/the-ancient-legend-of-hyperborea.html), he has restructured the political imagination of his homeland,
whose leader is
[paying](https://www.dw.de/opinion-putins-dangerous-eurasian-idea/a-17670509)
obvious
[attention](https://www.thejakartaglobe.com/opinion/vladimir-putin-rise-neo-eurasianism/). When history is integrated with myth, things can easily begin
to get
[exciting](https://www.theaustralian.com.au/news/world/russian-war-cries-herald-rise-of-george-orwells-eurasian-vision/story-fnb64oi6-1226890411626?nk=bc9ebef9472efb4e2fd1841dfc1c0036).

The fact that Atlantis is unmistakably
[sinking](https://www.uf-blog.net/quotable-27/) makes
the rising wolf-howls of Eurasianism all the more penetrating.
Decadence is a dilemma or a delight for those involved in it. For
those looking on, it is
[food](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2014/06/10/world/politics-diplomacy-world/putin-gambles-culture-war-west/#.U-Q-xvldWao). Eurasianism has the initiative, while the West
[reacts](https://theumlaut.com/2014/08/06/europes-neoreaction-is-scarier-than-you-think/).

The Eurasian Question, then, is not whether this ideology will
shake the world. That is already
[baked](https://www.project-syndicate.org/commentary/nouriel-roubini-maps-out-the-kremlin-s-plan-for-a-re-divided-world)
into the cake. The open question concerns
[China](https://www.cicir.ac.cn/english/ArticleView.aspx?nid=4092). In a re-ignited Hyperborean /
[Atlantean](https://mitrailleuse.net/2014/08/06/were-all-atlanteans-here/)
forever war, which way does China
[tilt](https://www.zerohedge.com/news/2014-07-27/china-cements-eurasian-trade-routes-great-silk-road-revival-infographic)?

For China, the ideological and geostrategic landscape opened by
the Eurasian challenge to the present global order offers
extraordinary
[leverage](https://www.atimes.com/atimes/China/CHIN-01-190514.html). A civilization that has
[long](https://www.chinaknowledge.de/History/Division/sanguo.html)
understood triangular
[diplomacy](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/culture/8512275/On-China-by-Henry-Kissinger-review.html)
as the optimal context for the exercise of strategic intelligence
can scarcely fail to find encouragement in this complex
[pattern](https://www.ibiblio.org/chinesehistory/contents/02cul/c03s03.html)
of widening fractures. In comparison to the cramped and dangerous
position of a world geostrategic challenger, that of a triangular
balancer presents advantages that are difficult to over-estimate.

China cannot plausibly be described as an ‘Atlantean’ power
(despite the great historical importance of its ‘[Singlosphere](https://www.hiebs.hku.hk/working_paper_updates/pdf/wp1071b.pdf)‘). Yet, neither is it ‘Hyperborean’ in any persuasive sense.
These options both belong to a dirempted Occident — understood
according to an expansive, rather than Eurasian definition,
attentive to common classical and Christian roots. The Eurasian
mythos is not inherently Sino-sensitive. China’s moves will be
made upon a still greater gaming table.

Both a (geographically) Eurasian and a Pacific-maritime power —
already, perhaps, a super-power — China has free options within
the conflicted global space that Dugin’s ideology so convincingly,
or at least compellingly, portrays. The next stage of Chinese
geopolitical evolution will occur within an environment of
dynamic, triangular tensions. The course of the world depends upon
how this opportunity is played.
