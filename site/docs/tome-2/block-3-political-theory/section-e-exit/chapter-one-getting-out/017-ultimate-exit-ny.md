# Ultimate Exit NY

<p class="article-meta">Tome II · The Dark Enlightenment / BLOCK 3 - POLITICAL THEORY / SECTION E - EXIT / CHAPTER ONE - GETTING OUT · December 6, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/ultimate-exit-ny/){ .article-source }

Some chatter on various web channels about
[this](https://www.bustler.net/index.php/event/ultimate_exit_the_architecture_and_urbanism_of_tech-secessionism/)
event, which should be a great opportunity for exploring. To be
clear about my participation (which has been open to confusion) —
it consists of an intervention out of Cyberspace. (No chance of
drinking dates in NY just yet, unfortunately.)

This is a nonlinear point, from my perspective, since the rapid
development of telepresence is of obvious internal consequence to
the recent intensification of Exit-oriented and neo-secessionist
discussion. (Balaji S. Srinivasan brought this out very clearly in
his October 2013
[talk](https://www.seasteading.org/2013/10/balaji-srinivasan-on-silicon-valleys-ultimate-exit/)
on the subject, from which this event takes its title.) Exit in
depth — i.e. into the crypto-thickened ‘Net — is at the very least
an important complement to more traditional notions of territorial
flight. It also sustains a better purchase on the commercial
principle which provides Exit with its fundamentalal model, and
which can easily get lost among secessionist excitement and
visions of technologically re-sculpted geographical space.

Some
[background](https://dismagazine.com/dystopia/evolved-lifestyles/67148/what-is-seasteading/)
to the event (and hints of choppy waters). Argument is, of course,
the other side of the nonlinearity (a micro-enactment of the
inclusive Democratic ideal), so it will be interesting to see
whether on this occasion the controversy can remain productive in
its own terms, rather than ‘merely’ stacking up the incentives to
get Out.
