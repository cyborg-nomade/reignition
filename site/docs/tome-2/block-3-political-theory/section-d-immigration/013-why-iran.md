[is](https://www.scottaaronson.com/blog/?p=3167), and the
title of this post isn’t even centrally his question, so I’m asking

If you were trying to discredit a demographic policy that
discriminated against Islamization, the thing rolled out by the US
administration looks like a good way to do it. Shouldn’t selecting
against Salafism be the policy core? Such a stance could be easily
based upon solid American
[precedent](https://gborjas.org/2016/08/16/on-vetting-immigrants/).
[This](https://www.niacaction.org/what-does-trumps-ban-on-iranians-and-muslims-mean-for-you/)

is to say hastily hashed-up populism food.)
