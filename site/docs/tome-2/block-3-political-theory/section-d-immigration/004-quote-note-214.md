# Quote note (#214)

<p class="article-meta">Tome II · The Dark Enlightenment / BLOCK 3 - POLITICAL THEORY / SECTION D - IMMIGRATION · February 1, 2016</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/quote-note-214/){ .article-source }

The intolerable clarity of Sailer at
[work](https://takimag.com/article/diversity_vs_solidarity_steve_sailer/page_2#axzz3yu1WACLZ):

*… the concept of “Europeanism” upon which the EU was founded —
that Europeans should be more neighborly to their fellow Europeans
than to non-Europeans — is increasingly unmentionable in polite
society because it’s seen as racist. For example, during the peak
of adulation for Merkel before reality set in, she was widely
praised for personifying European values by de-Europeanizing
Europe.*

How would one even begin to argue with anything said here? There’s a
lot in this short passage, but nothing that isn’t obviously true,
*to everyone*, which accounts — perhaps — for the fact that
it is nevertheless almost unthinkably controversial.

It would be a relief to see Merkel awarded the 2016 Nobel Peace
Prize for her contribution to the
[ruin](https://sputniknews.com/europe/20160105/1032701256/angela-merkel-refugees-eu.html)
of Europe. If not honest — or anything close — it would at least
attain meta-honesty, by defining ‘peace’ explicitly as the
suppression of truth.
