# Interesting Times

<p class="article-meta">Tome I · Urban Future / BLOCK 3 - BITCOIN AND BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGY / CHAPTER THREE - BTC POLITICS · July 24, 2015</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.uf-blog.net/interesting-times/){ .article-source }

Blockchain schizophrenia is reaching
[criticality](https://www.nasdaq.com/article/the-cryptocold-war-bitcoins-politics-of-scalability-cm497963):

*So we find ourselves in the Bitcoin “[missile crisis](https://qntra.net/2015/01/the-hard-fork-missile-crisis/),” and uncomfortable ironies abound. The decentralized currency
is beset by centralizing pressures if it changes or if it doesn’t.
The apolitical currency is being rent by a deeply political rift
between camps, each of which purports to be the trusted authority
over the trustless, anti-authoritarian currency.*

No one ever said anarchistic collective decision-making was going to
be easy.
