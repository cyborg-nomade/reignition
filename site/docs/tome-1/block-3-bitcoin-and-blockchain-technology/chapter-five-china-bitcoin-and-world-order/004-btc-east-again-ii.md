# BTC East (again) II

<p class="article-meta">Tome I · Urban Future / BLOCK 3 - BITCOIN AND BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGY / CHAPTER FIVE - CHINA, BITCOIN AND WORLD ORDER · March 11, 2015</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.uf-blog.net/btc-east-again-ii/){ .article-source }

The world isn’t
[cooperating](https://qz.com/359679/80-of-bitcoin-is-exchanged-into-and-out-of-chinese-yuan/)
with those who want to think about one thing at a time:

*In a report out today from Goldman Sachs about the future of
money, the bank points out that
**80% of bitcoin volume is now exchanged into and out of Chinese
yuan**. The second-highest trading currency is the US dollar, followed
by smaller denominations in yen and euros.*

The fate of Bitcoin is inextricable from that of the global monetary
order, and coming at it from
[that](https://www.uf-blog.net/hegemonic-headaches/)
direction is increasingly unavoidable.
