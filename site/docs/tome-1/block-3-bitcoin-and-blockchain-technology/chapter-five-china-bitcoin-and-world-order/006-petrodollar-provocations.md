# Petrodollar Provocations

<p class="article-meta">Tome I · Urban Future / BLOCK 3 - BITCOIN AND BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGY / CHAPTER FIVE - CHINA, BITCOIN AND WORLD ORDER · July 2, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.uf-blog.net/petrodollar-provocations/){ .article-source }

The mere fact
[this](https://www.gulf-times.com/eco.-bus.%20news/256/details/398622/kuwait-finance-firm-suggests-trading-oil-in-bitcoins)
conversation is even happening has to be disturbing to some
extremely powerful global interests. BTC volatility appears to be
the only major obstacle to the cryptocurrency’s widespread
international adoption at this point. If it trends downwards, a
switch point will be suggested on the horizon. In the interim, the
BTC option sets implicit limits to USD devaluation — the cost of
volatility isn’t infinite.

The article expects China to oppose any move to price oil in BTC in
global markets, based on ambitions for an expanded international use
of the RMB. Given what Chinese monetary authorities
[know](https://www.bis.org/review/r090402c.pdf) about the
Triffin
[dilemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triffin_dilemma),
this is an argument that can very easily be over-stretched.
