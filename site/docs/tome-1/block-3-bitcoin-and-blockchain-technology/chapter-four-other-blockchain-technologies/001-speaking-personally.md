# Speaking Personally …

<p class="article-meta">Tome I · Urban Future / BLOCK 3 - BITCOIN AND BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGY / CHAPTER FOUR - OTHER BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGIES · December 19, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.uf-blog.net/speaking-personally/){ .article-source }

Under the compulsion of formality, complex legal-administrative
codes have no option but to make space for the future.

FinCEN’s crucial (and still incompletely digested) guidance
[note](https://fincen.gov/statutes_regs/guidance/html/FIN-2013-G001.html)
on virtual currencies, issued March 18, 2013, clarifies in a
footnote (#2):

*FinCEN’s regulations define “person” as “an individual, a
corporation, a partnership, a trust or estate, a joint stock
company, an association, a syndicate, joint venture, or other
unincorporated organization or group, an Indian Tribe (as that
term is defined in the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act), and all
entities cognizable as legal personalities.”*

There’s plenty of room already for almost anything to slither in.
