# Bitcoin vs Leviathan

<p class="article-meta">Tome I · Urban Future / BLOCK 3 - BITCOIN AND BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGY / CHAPTER TWO - BTC DEATH? · March 1, 2013</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/bitcoin-vs-leviathan/){ .article-source }

Moldbug’s
[prediction](https://unqualified-reservations.blogspot.com/2013/01/how-bitcoin-dies.html): Freedom loses (as usual).

The question is this: Which dominates? The malignancy of Leviathan,
or its incompetence? How radically can the metastatic cancer-phase
State shape reality in conformity to its vision?

Bitcoin — which is essentially an experiment in Austrian monetary
theory — provides the model test-bed in which this question can be
lucidly decided. Its current
[rising](https://www.zerohedge.com/news/2013-02-26/alternative-currency-goes-mainstream-bitcoin-atms-emerge)
[fortunes](https://www.zerohedge.com/news/2013-02-28/bitcoin-your-thoughts)
only accelerates the decision. If Bitcoin can’t be stopped,
Leviathan is exposed as a paper tiger.

The best way to make the bet, of course, is to buy (or short) BTC.
*Outside in* has been too apathetic to put resources behind
its hunches yet, but (for the zilch it’s worth) our intuitions run
*contra* Moldbug on the topic. Compared to Cyberspace, where
bitcoin is entrenched, the State is weak, unintelligent, uninformed,
parochial, poorly designed, and — in each respect — getting ever
more so, in both comparative and absolute terms. The truly
stupendous idiocy of Leviathan thoroughly swamps its evil, as is
demonstrated every time it tries to get something done.

The digital Outside, in contrast, is already far beyond recall. The
germ of a free economy is under construction.
