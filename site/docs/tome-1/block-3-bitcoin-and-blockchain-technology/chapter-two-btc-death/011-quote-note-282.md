# Quote note (#282)

<p class="article-meta">Tome I · Urban Future / BLOCK 3 - BITCOIN AND BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGY / CHAPTER TWO - BTC DEATH? · September 9, 2016</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/quote-note-282/){ .article-source }

At least superficially, under-funding is the strict reciprocal of
[hype](https://www.bloomberg.com/gadfly/articles/2016-09-08/blockchain-s-ambitions-thwarted-by-funding-resources):

*The blockchain industry is either hugely under-resourced or
hugely over-optimistic. Probably both.*

Bitcoin rigorously formalizes the common insight that
*words are cheap* (it emerged out of spam-filter solutions).
So this analysis is intriguingly ironic, as well as obviously
thought-provoking.
