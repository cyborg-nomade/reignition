Photography is forbidden in the Dunhuang grottoes, and under the
close supervision of the mandatory tour, this prohibition is
strictly enforced. Photography is also forbidden in the adjacent
Mogaoku Museum …

The spine of the museum consists of a row of (extremely impressive)
cave reconstructions, sampled from among the 492 decorated caves at
the site. (A two-hour tour of the site takes in perhaps 10.)

The following images are of reconstructions, not originals. The
photographic quality is especially dire, given the unusual lighting
conditions and cramped space. What I’m posting here is what I’ve
got. (Click on images to expand.)
