The caves shown are in the northern cluster, whose exterior features
have not been defaced by reinforced concrete. The southern group has
been externally ruined by Zhou Enlai (although he seems to have
meant well), but its interiors are the great treasures of the site,
and some are open to the public, by guided tour. Some images of
southern cave interiors (reconstructions) to follow.
