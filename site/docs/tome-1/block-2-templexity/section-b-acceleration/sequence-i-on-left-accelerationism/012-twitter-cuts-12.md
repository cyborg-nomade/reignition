In the admittedly oddly-angled opinion of this blog, the final
tweet in this sequence is the most theoretically significant
statement of Left Accelerationist purpose since the 19th century.
Attenuation of socially necessary labor time has to be
arithmetically integrated with the concept of ‘acceleration’ for a
seam of Marxian continuity to be pursued.
