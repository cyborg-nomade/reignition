# The Left Turn

<p class="article-meta">Tome I · Urban Future / BLOCK 2 - TEMPLEXITY / SECTION B - ACCELERATION / SEQUENCE i - ON LEFT ACCELERATIONISM · July 17, 2014</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.uf-blog.net/the-left-turn/){ .article-source }

Left Accelerationism undergoes further consolidation, assisted by
two high-quality posts, from
[Fractal Ontology](https://fractalontology.wordpress.com/2014/07/16/technoscience-and-expressionism/)
and
[Deontologistics](https://deontologistics.tumblr.com/post/91953882443/so-accelerationism-whats-all-that-about).

Since Left-framing is a transcendental condition of publicization
in the present world order, UF is encouraged to see it being done
well. The implications of this development are inextricable from
the core controversy at issue: Can acceleration be extracted from
its capitalist matrix for socialist redeployment? Left
Accelerationists, confident that this is possible, are setting out
to demonstrate it. Right Accelerationists, no less confident of
its impossibility, have no incentive to obstruct them.

If capital can be exceeded, it deserves to be (by Natural Law). If
it cannot, strenuous efforts to exceed it produce tangled
elaborations of its potencies. Complexity, competition, pressure,
and experimentation are what accelerationism is for.
