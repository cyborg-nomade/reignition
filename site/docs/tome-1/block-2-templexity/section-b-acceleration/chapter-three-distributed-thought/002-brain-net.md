# Brain-Net

<p class="article-meta">Tome I · Urban Future / BLOCK 2 - TEMPLEXITY / SECTION B - ACCELERATION / CHAPTER THREE - DISTRIBUTED THOUGHT · July 20, 2015</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.uf-blog.net/brain-net/){ .article-source }

… and suddenly, the age of the networked brain has
[arrived](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2015/jul/09/monkey-brain-net-raises-prospect-of-human-brain-to-brain-connection):

*Miguel Nicolelis, the Duke University scientist behind the
work, has
[previously pioneered](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2014/apr/01/mind-controlled-robotic-suit-exoskeleton-world-cup-2014)
the development of brain-machine interfaces that could allow
amputees and paralysed people to directly control prosthetic
limbs and exoskeletons. His latest advance may have clinical
benefits in brain rehabilitation, he predicts, but could also
pave the way for “organic computers” – collectives of animal
brains linked together to solve problems. […] “Essentially we
created a super-brain,” he said. “A collective brain created
from three monkey brains. Nobody has ever done that before.” […]
He dismissed comparisons with science fiction plots, however,
saying: “We’re conditioned by movies and Hollywood to think that
everything related to science is dangerous and scary. These
scary scenarios never crossed my mind and I’m the one doing the
experiments.”*

Neural
[interface](https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/computers_math/neural_interfaces/)
technology has been hurtling forwards
[recently](https://www.wired.co.uk/news/archive/2015-06/09/flexible-electronics-injected-into-the-brain). The step from lunatic science fiction speculation to
established technoscientific procedure is increasingly taken in
advance of any engaged discussion, without an interval for serious
social reflection. That’s acceleration as it concretely happens.
It’s not a new topic for prolonged thought, it’s the fact that the
time for prolonged thought — and its associated space for
collective ethico-political consideration — is no longer ever
going to be available.
