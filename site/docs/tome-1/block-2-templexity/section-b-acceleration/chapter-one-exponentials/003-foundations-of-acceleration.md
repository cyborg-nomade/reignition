# Foundations of Acceleration

<p class="article-meta">Tome I · Urban Future / BLOCK 2 - TEMPLEXITY / SECTION B - ACCELERATION / CHAPTER ONE - EXPONENTIALS · January 27, 2015</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.uf-blog.net/foundations-of-acceleration/){ .article-source }

For the intellectual-historical foundations of Accelerationism
there’s one obvious
[place](https://www.urbanomic.com/pub_accelerate.php) to
go.

A search for its conceptual foundations, however, allows of short
cuts.
[This](https://intelligence.org/files/IEM.pdf) is one of
them (and an extraordinarily valuable one).

Yudkowsky does not write of ‘acceleration’ but of “returns on
cognitive reinvestment” as the basic problem of “intelligence
explosion microeconomics”. The topic is quite clearly
*identical*.

The explosion of ethico-political anguish around the
Accelerationist thesis tends to obscure the fundamental conceptual
issues. This paper is a crucial corrective.
