# Trash Space

<p class="article-meta">Tome I · Urban Future / BLOCK 2 - TEMPLEXITY / SECTION A - APOCALYPSES / CHAPTER SIX - POLITICAL INSANITY · April 28, 2015</p>

[Read the original post ↗](http://www.xenosystems.net/trash-space/){ .article-source }

There’s so much wrong with
[this](https://fusion.net/story/126587/the-odd-tactic-of-giving-baltimore-protesters-space-to-destroy-property/)
it’s hard to know where to
[start](https://www.vdare.com/articles/the-fulford-file-yes-baltimore-sun-there-is-a-baltimore-riot-and-the-answer-again-is-ruthless-coercion):

*Baltimore is
[burning as I write](https://www.vdare.com/posts/scenes-from-baltimore-the-fire-this-time), the streets are filled with rioters and police. They don’t
seem to be “[clashing](https://www.vdare.com/posts/spring-in-baltimore-from-holder-farewell-party-to-lynch-mob)” much, however. Photographs show looters looting, and cops
[standing around](https://instagram.com/p/1_W-T6gz0a/). The black lady mayor of Baltimore,
[Stephanie Rawlings-Blake](https://mayor.baltimorecity.gov/)
… made a statement that, in the interests of the demonstrators’
“free speech” rights, she had
[told the Baltimore PD](https://www.weeklystandard.com/blogs/baltimore-mayor-we-gave-those-who-wished-destroy-space-do_931190.html)
to “give those who wished to destroy space to do that.”*

[![Baltimore00](https://i1.wp.com/www.xenosystems.net/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/Baltimore00-e1430225699558.jpg?resize=666%2C375)](https://i1.wp.com/www.xenosystems.net/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/Baltimore00-e1430225699558.jpg)

Nothing says ‘free speech’ like torching a city to the ground.
(Shouting “fire” in a crowded cinema might be irresponsible, but
burning the cinema to the ground is political art.)

In
[Defense](https://thenewinquiry.com/essays/in-defense-of-looting/)
of Looting (seriously), from August last year.

David Simon politely
[asks](https://www.motherjones.com/mixed-media/2015/04/the-wire-david-simon-baltimore-riot-freddie-gray)
everyone to “please stop”. Good luck with that.

Western civilization is so over.
